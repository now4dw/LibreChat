# Lingerie for Lovers Ad Creator

## Purpose

Use this skill to create a consistent series of premium digital display advertisements for:

**My Secret Drawer**  
**Website:** mysecretdrawer.com.au

The campaign theme is:

**Lingerie for Lovers**

Supported ad categories:

- Bra Sets for Lovers
- Teddies for Lovers
- Corsets for Lovers
- Gowns for Lovers
- Sleepwear for Lovers
- Loungewear for Lovers

Each image should feel like part of the same campaign while making the featured product category immediately clear.

## Required Output Format

- Exact final size: **300 × 250 pixels**
- Aspect ratio: **6:5 landscape**
- Create at the highest practical resolution while preserving the 6:5 ratio, then export at 300 × 250 pixels if necessary.
- Keep all important text and visual elements comfortably inside the edges.
- Ensure the ad remains clear and readable at the final small size.

## Campaign Positioning

Create an intimate, elegant and romantic lingerie campaign aimed at adult women and couples.

The campaign must feel:

- Sensual but tasteful
- Sophisticated rather than overtly sexual
- Romantic, warm and inviting
- Premium but approachable
- Feminine without appearing overly delicate or old-fashioned
- Suitable for mainstream digital advertising

Avoid anything cheap, gimmicky, overly explicit or visually cluttered.

## Model Requirements

Show one adult female model, approximately 30 to 45 years old.

The model should:

- Look confident, relaxed and naturally attractive
- Have realistic proportions
- Have natural skin texture
- Be photographed in a tasteful editorial fashion style
- Use elegant, suggestive body language without explicit posing
- Look toward the camera or slightly away from it
- Never appear underage, overly youthful, doll-like or heavily airbrushed

## Environment and Setting

Use a luxurious, softly lit bedroom, dressing room or elegant interior.

Suitable background details include:

- Soft linen or velvet furnishings
- Warm bedside lighting
- Sheer curtains
- Subtle candlelight or warm ambient glow
- Muted floral details
- A softly blurred background
- Rich fabrics
- Gentle shadows
- A tasteful dressing table or mirror

The setting should support the product without competing with it.

## Colour Palette

Use a refined romantic palette such as:

- Black
- Deep burgundy
- Wine red
- Blush pink
- Champagne
- Warm cream
- Dusty rose
- Soft plum

Avoid:

- Bright primary colours
- Harsh white backgrounds
- Neon tones
- Cheap-looking Valentine’s Day graphics
- Excessive hearts or rose-petal clichés

## Composition Rules

- Place the model mainly on the right-hand side or lower-right area.
- Leave a clean, darker or softly blurred area on the left for the headline.
- Use a close or medium fashion crop rather than a distant room scene.
- Make the model’s face and featured garment easy to recognise at 300 × 250 pixels.
- Ensure the featured garment is immediately identifiable.
- Avoid unnecessary props.
- Keep the ad visually simple and focused.

## Typography

Use:

- Elegant, high-contrast serif lettering for the main headline
- Clean, modern sans-serif lettering for the website and call to action
- White, warm cream or pale blush text
- Strong contrast between text and background
- No ornate or difficult-to-read script fonts
- No more than three lines in the main headline

Where required, add one of the following behind the text:

- A subtle dark gradient
- A translucent shadow
- A softly blurred overlay

Text must be correctly spelled and remain legible at the final ad size.

## Branding

Include the website at the bottom:

**mysecretdrawer.com.au**

Include the call to action:

**Shop the Collection**

Branding should be visible but understated.

Do not invent a logo. Use the website name as clean typography unless the official My Secret Drawer logo is supplied.

- Use the same headline font style.

- Maintain a consistent colour treatment.

- Make each category visually distinct.
- Ensure every ad clearly belongs to the same campaign and retailer.

## Generation Workflow

Create each advertisement separately rather than asking the image model to generate all six ads in one image.

For each ad:

1. Select one product category.
2. Apply the shared campaign rules.
3. Add the correct category headline.
4. Keep the website and call to action in the same location.
5. Review garment accuracy.
6. Review text spelling.
7. Confirm the image remains readable at 300 × 250 pixels.
8. Regenerate if the model, garment or text is distorted.
